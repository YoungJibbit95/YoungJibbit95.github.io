## Evidence contract

- Three distinct concepts, all genuine browser renders; at least two use R3F.
- Comparable viewport pairs: 1440×900 and 390×844. Also show a real discovery
  and the Origin interpretation, not just a static hero image.
- Installed stack only. Self-authored procedural geometry/materials are explicitly
  illustrative; project claims and source links reuse pinned G2-C evidence.
- Real camera motion, an actionable discovery, keyboard/touch alternatives,
  readable DOM information and reduced motion. Record errors, renderer workload,
  idle demand frames and prototype limitations separately from production gates.
- Full engineering gates apply to any integrated technical patch. Pixel evidence
  does not grant visual approval. Ask the user to choose only after concrete,
  reviewable desktop/mobile comparisons exist.
- Keep raw traces outside Git where reproducibly archived; do not destroy older
  evidence. Original ZIP and local unassigned lockfile metadata remain untouched.
